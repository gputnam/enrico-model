# MINERvA medium-energy (ME) FHC flux, ν–e scattering constrained, 2019

**Source:** E. Valencia *et al.* (MINERvA), "Constraint of the MINERvA medium energy neutrino flux using neutrino-electron elastic scattering", *Phys. Rev. D* **100**, 092001 (2019), [arXiv:1906.00111](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.00111).
**Files:** downloaded unmodified from the arXiv ancillary files, https://arxiv.org/src/1906.00111v4/anc/. `AAA_README.txt` is the collaboration's description.

**Contents:** `MINERvA_Flux_pdg{14,-14,12,-12}_500MeVBins.csv` have the columns `BinLow(GeV),BinHigh(GeV),Flux(neutrinos/m^2/POT/GeV)`. There are 0.5 GeV bins from 0 to 100 GeV. The flux is that in the fiducial volume of the scintillator tracker after the ν–e constraint.

**Use in nuflow:** `format: csv, density: per_GeV`.
